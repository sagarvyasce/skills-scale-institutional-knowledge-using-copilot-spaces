# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative project management process that moves work from initiation and planning into execution, release, and continuous improvement. During initiation, teams define the problem, goals, stakeholders, and success metrics. Planning then turns approved work into prioritized backlog items, milestones, dependencies, and a release approach.

Execution and tracking focus on small PRs, regular team rhythms, quality checks, and visible progress against milestones. Risk management and communication are embedded throughout delivery through a maintained risk register, recurring updates, and clear escalation paths. Release and deployment guidance emphasizes CI/security checks, rollback readiness, and post-deploy verification.

Retrospectives close each sprint, release, or milestone by capturing what worked, what did not, and concrete follow-up actions. Roles and personas across Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA, and Stakeholders provide clear ownership and collaboration expectations at every phase.
